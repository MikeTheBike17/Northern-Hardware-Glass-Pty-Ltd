What Is Laminated Glass

A layered safety glazing solution for modern residential, commercial, and specialist projects.

Laminated glass consists of two or more panes bonded together with one or more polyvinyl butyral interlayers. If accidental impact occurs, the glass can crack while the interlayer helps hold the pieces in place, reducing fallout and improving safety.

Depending on the configuration, laminated glass can also improve acoustic insulation, help manage solar energy, support security requirements, and unlock tailored finishes such as tinted, reflective, Low-E, decorative, or structural assemblies.

Typical Advantages

  • Safer breakage behavior with retained fragments.
  • Extra resistance against forced entry compared with standard float glass.
  • Available in multiple colours, coatings, and performance-driven interlayers.
  • Useful for UV reduction, sound control, and improved comfort inside buildings.
  • Can be specified for everything from doors and windows to skylights and security counters.
