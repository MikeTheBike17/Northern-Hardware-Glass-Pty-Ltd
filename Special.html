What We Manufacture

Specialised glass products built around non-standard requirements.

In addition to standard supply, we work with architects, designers, fabricators, restorers, and industrial clients to produce glass for applications where off-the-shelf products are not suitable. That includes unusual shapes, demanding operating environments, heritage replacements, and decorative or safety-led custom work.

If you have a concept, drawing, technical reference, or an existing glass sample, our team can help turn it into a finished product that matches the function, appearance, and manufacturing requirements of the brief.

Project Capabilities

  • Unusual sizes, curves, and complex geometries for one-off or repeat supply.
  • Decorative, safety-critical, impact-resistant, and restoration-led glazing solutions.
  • Custom cuts, edge finishes, cut-outs, and colour-coordinated interior glass options.
  • Replacement glass for hard-to-source or discontinued products and equipment.
  • Precision manufacturing focused on quality, reliable lead times, and application-specific performance.
