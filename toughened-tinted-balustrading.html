Toughened Product Detail

Tinted Balustrading - Flat & Curved

Tinted toughened balustrading combines the strength of safety glass with a more expressive architectural finish. It is well suited to projects that need shading, privacy, glare softening, or a stronger design statement without losing the openness of glass.

Tinted FinishPrivacy + Glare ControlFlat + Curved

Overview

This product brings together the structural reliability of toughened safety glass and the aesthetic benefits of tinted glazing. It can help reduce harsh light, soften views, and support more privacy than fully clear glass while still maintaining a refined, minimal balustrade system.

Flat and curved forms can both be produced depending on the project geometry, making the product suitable for premium residential work, commercial entrances, and lifestyle spaces with a stronger design focus.

Key benefits

  • Adds colour depth and visual character to the balustrade design.
  • Supports improved glare control and a measure of additional privacy.
  • Retains the strength and safer breakage behaviour of toughened glass.
  • Suitable for both straight runs and curved architectural lines.
  • Pairs well with polished edges and contemporary fixing systems.

Typical Applications

  • Luxury residential balconies
  • Hotel and hospitality stairways
  • Pool surrounds and leisure decks
  • Feature guarding in commercial spaces

Specification Note

Tint selection, visible light transmission, and glass thickness should be aligned with the project's performance goals, hardware system, and overall architectural palette.

Thickness + Processing

Available toughened glass thicknesses

Can be custom cut, polished and processed to size. The same standard toughened glass thicknesses and curved production limits apply to tinted balustrading builds.

Straight toughened glass sheet thicknesses

  • 4mm up to 2400 x 1800mm
  • 5mm up to 2400 x 4200mm
  • 6mm up to 2400 x 4200mm
  • 8mm up to 2400 x 4200mm
  • 10mm up to 2400 x 4200mm
  • 12mm up to 2400 x 4200mm
  • 15mm up to 2400 x 3210mm
  • 19mm up to 2400 x 3210mm

Curved toughened glass capacity

  • Maximum height: 2400mm
  • Maximum width: 1800mm
  • Minimum radius: 800mm
  • Processing: Edge finishing, polishing, and custom sizing available.
