Low-E Laminated Glass

Low-E laminated glass combines laminated safety construction with a thin transparent coating that reflects heat while allowing daylight to pass through. It is designed for projects that need safer glazing and stronger thermal performance in the same assembly.

Energy EfficiencyThermal ComfortSafety Glazing

Overview

By reflecting a portion of infrared energy, Low-E laminated glass helps moderate internal temperatures and reduce dependence on mechanical heating and cooling. It is especially valuable for projects that want better year-round comfort while still meeting glazing safety needs.

The Low-E coating can be combined with laminated construction for a more complete performance package, making this option appealing for both contemporary homes and commercial buildings with large glazed areas.

Key benefits

  • Reflects heat to help keep interiors cooler in summer and warmer in winter.
  • Maintains visible light transmission while managing solar performance.
  • Supports better energy efficiency across the building envelope.
  • Retains laminated safety and security characteristics.
  • Helps protect interiors by filtering UV.

Typical Applications

  • Modern homes with large glazed openings
  • Commercial facades and offices
  • Projects targeting better indoor comfort and energy performance
  • Glazing systems combining safety and solar control

Specification Note

Coating position, glass make-up, and framing system should all be reviewed together to achieve the required thermal result.
