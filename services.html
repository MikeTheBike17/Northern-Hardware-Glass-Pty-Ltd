Why choose Northern Hardware & Glass (Pty) Ltd?


Experience ▾

Read

Established in 1978, Northern Hardware & Glass (Pty) Ltd brings decades of hands-on experience in the glass manufacturing industry. With a proven footprint of 12 branches, we have built a reputation for delivering reliable, high-quality products and services to a diverse customer base across residential, commercial, industrial and specialised sectors. Our longevity in the industry reflects more than technical capability — it speaks to strong, long-term customer relationships built on trust, consistency and practical problem-solving. We work closely with clients to understand their specific requirements, offering personalised guidance and technical input throughout each project.

Innovation ▾

Read

Innovation is central to the way Northern Hardware & Glass evolves. From the craftsmanship of our Cottage Collection (Moulda Glass) to the precision engineering behind modern double glazing systems and custom-manufactured mirrors, uluminium mirrors and reflective glass, we continue to adapt to changing market needs and advancing technologies. By combining traditional techniques with contemporary manufacturing processes, we are able to deliver products that balance quality, performance and durability. Our ongoing investment in skills, equipment and product development enables us to offer solutions that meet today’s energy efficiency, safety and design expectations, while still honouring the quality standards that have defined our business for generations.

Quality Standards ▾

Read

Northern Hardware & Glass (Pty) Ltd are permit holders of the South African Global Conformity Services (SABS). We are also a member of the South African Glass and Glazing Association (SAGGA) as well as AAMSA.

End-to-End Service ▾

Read

From initial design consultation and accurate on-site measuring through to precision manufacturing and professional installation, we provide a complete end-to-end service. Our team remains closely involved at every stage of the process, ensuring clear communication, attention to detail and solutions tailored to each project. This integrated approach allows us to deliver consistent quality, reliable timelines and results that meet both functional and aesthetic expectations — your satisfaction remains our promise.



More About Us

Northern Hardware & Glass (Pty) Ltd has proudly served South Africa’s glass industry since 1978. As a trusted industry leader, we have grown into a national operation with 12 branches specialising in retail, wholesale, and manufacturing, delivering solutions that meet the highest standards of quality and performance. Our commitment to continuous innovation drives us to expand our product offering, including advanced mirror ranges, specialised architectural glass, and bespoke solutions tailored to unique applications. From frameless shower enclosures and shopfronts to safety glass, moulded glass, and precision-engineered installations, every product is crafted with clarity, durability, and safety in mind. We pride ourselves on a hands-on, client-focused approach, working closely with customers on specialised projects such as custom boat glazing, specialised vehicle glass, agricultural installations, and construction-grade windows. Every solution is designed to meet exact specifications, ensuring optimal performance in demanding environments. At Northern Hardware & Glass (Pty) Ltd, quality is not a benchmark — it is a promise. Your satisfaction, backed by decades of expertise and innovation, remains at the heart of everything we do.
