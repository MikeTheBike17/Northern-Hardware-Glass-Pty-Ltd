Laminated Glass Products

Our laminated glass range combines high safety performance, modern design and long-lasting durability. From homes and offices to high-security environments, we manufacture laminated glass to suit your exact requirements.

Why Choose Laminated Glass?

  • Improved safety – glass stays in place when broken.
  • Better security – harder to penetrate than standard glass.
  • Noise, UV and heat reduction options available.
  • Wide choice of tints, coatings and thicknesses.

Laminated Safety Glass – Clear

Transparent laminated safety glass is manufactured by bonding two or more layers of glass together with a tough, durable interlayer. This construction ensures that if the glass breaks, the fragments remain adhered to the interlayer instead of falling free, significantly reducing the risk of injury.

Clear laminated glass offers the perfect balance between maximum visibility and enhanced safety, making it a popular choice for both residential and commercial applications.


  • Ideal for doors, windows, shopfronts, balustrades, and interior partitions where safety and clarity are essential.
  • Maintains excellent optical clarity while providing built-in safety performance.
  • Helps block harmful UV rays that can cause fading of furnishings, flooring, and displays.
  • Improves acoustic insulation by reducing noise transmission through the glass.
  • Adds an extra layer of security, making it more difficult to penetrate compared to standard float glass.

Laminated Safety Glass – Tinted

Tinted laminated safety glass is produced by bonding two or more tinted glass layers with a strong interlayer, combining safety performance with improved solar control and privacy. In the event of breakage, the glass fragments remain bonded to the interlayer, reducing the risk of injury.

Tinted laminated glass is ideal for applications where heat reduction, glare control, and visual comfort are required, while still maintaining the safety benefits of laminated glass.


  • Suitable for windows, doors, façades, balustrades, and large glazed areas exposed to direct sunlight.
  • Reduces glare and solar heat gain, helping to improve indoor comfort.
  • Enhances privacy without completely blocking natural light.
  • Provides UV protection to help prevent fading of interior furnishings and flooring.
  • Provides the same safety and security advantages as clear laminated glass.
  • Available in a range of modern tint colours.

Laminated Safety Glass – Rainbow

Rainbow laminated safety glass is manufactured by laminating coloured or decorative interlayers between glass panes, creating vibrant visual effects while retaining the strength and safety characteristics of laminated glass. If broken, the glass remains held together by the interlayer for added safety.

This type of laminated glass is designed for architectural, decorative, and feature applications, where aesthetics are as important as performance.


  • Ideal for feature walls, partitions, façades, balustrades, and artistic installations.
  • Offers unique colour effects and light diffusion depending on lighting conditions.
  • Maintains the safety performance required for laminated glass applications.
  • Provides UV filtering to help protect interiors and materials.
  • Allows designers to combine creativity with safety and durability.
  • Available in a range of modern colours.

Low-E Laminated Glass

Low-E (Low Emissivity) laminated glass combines laminated safety glass with a microscopically thin, transparent coating that reflects heat while allowing natural light to pass through. This technology improves thermal performance without compromising clarity or safety.

Low-E laminated glass is designed to enhance energy efficiency and indoor comfort, making it ideal for modern residential and commercial buildings.


  • Reduces heat transfer by reflecting infrared energy, helping to keep interiors cooler in summer and warmer in winter.
  • Allows high levels of visible light transmission while limiting solar heat gain.
  • Improves overall energy efficiency and reduces reliance on heating and cooling systems.
  • Provides UV filtering to help protect interiors from fading.
  • Retains the safety and security benefits of laminated glass construction.

Sound Reducing Acoustic Glass

Sound reducing acoustic glass is a type of laminated glass manufactured using a specialised acoustic interlayer designed to dampen sound vibrations. This significantly reduces the transmission of noise through the glass while maintaining safety and clarity.

Acoustic laminated glass is ideal for environments where noise control, comfort, and privacy are essential.


  • Reduces noise transmission by approximately 30–45 dB, depending on glass thickness and configuration.
  • Particularly effective at reducing traffic noise, voices, aircraft noise, and urban sound pollution.
  • Suitable for homes, offices, studios, hotels, hospitals, and buildings near busy roads or airports.
  • Maintains the safety performance of laminated glass, with fragments adhering to the interlayer if broken.
  • Is available to be combined with tinted or Low-E coatings for added solar and thermal performance.

Custom Curved Laminated Glass

Custom curved laminated glass is manufactured by carefully forming glass layers to precise radii before laminating them with a strong interlayer. This process allows for complex curved designs while maintaining the safety, strength, and durability of laminated glass.

Curved laminated glass is ideal for architectural features and bespoke design elements where aesthetics and safety must work together.


  • Suitable for curved façades, staircases, balustrades, canopies, furniture, and feature installations.
  • Enables smooth, continuous curves without compromising structural integrity.
  • Maintains laminated safety performance, with glass fragments adhering to the interlayer if broken.
  • Available to be combined with clear, tinted, Low-E, or acoustic interlayers to meet performance requirements.

Laminated Reflective Glass

Laminated reflective glass combines laminated safety glass with a reflective coating designed to control solar heat and glare while enhancing privacy. The laminated construction ensures safety by holding glass fragments together if breakage occurs.

Reflective laminated glass is commonly used in commercial and high-exposure architectural applications where solar control and visual impact are important.

Multi Laminated Glass for Walkways & Skylights

Multi laminated glass for walkways and skylights is engineered using multiple layers of glass bonded with high-strength interlayers to provide enhanced load-bearing capacity and safety. This construction ensures that even if breakage occurs, the glass remains intact and continues to offer protection.

This type of laminated glass is specifically designed for overhead and walk-on applications, where safety, durability, and performance are critical.

Bullet Resistant Glass

Bullet resistant glass is manufactured by laminating multiple layers of glass and specialised interlayers to absorb and disperse the energy of ballistic impact. This layered construction significantly increases resistance to penetration while maintaining visibility and safety.

Bullet resistant glass is used in high-security applications where protection against forced entry and ballistic threats is required.


  • Designed to resist various levels of ballistic impact depending on thickness and configuration.
  • Commonly used in banks, retail counters, guardhouses, vehicles, government buildings, and secure facilities.
  • Maintains structural integrity after impact, reducing the risk of penetration.
  • Provides additional protection against smash-and-grab attacks.
  • Available in different specifications to meet security and safety requirements of our clients.

What is Laminated Glass?

Laminated glass consists of two or more panels of glass bonded together by an interlayer of one or more sheets of polyvinyl butyral (PVB), which provides a high degree of safety. When accidental impact occurs, the glass will shatter, but will be held intact by the PVB interlayer.

Environmental benefits of laminated glass:
The configuration and nature of certain laminated glass provides sound reducing insulation e.g. glass enclosed restaurants over roads or other architectural applications. In addition, our laminated glass is also available in different thicknesses (HPR & HPI), various colours and reflective glass, to provide greater energy efficiency for buildings by controlling solar energy. Other laminated safety glass products available: Bullet resistant glass – in various thicknesses to comply to ballistic ratings. Toughened laminated glass – to provide high structural strength and integrity.
