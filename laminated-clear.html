Laminated Safety Glass - Clear

Clear laminated safety glass is made by bonding two or more layers of glass together with a durable interlayer. If the glass breaks, the fragments remain adhered to that interlayer, helping reduce fallout and improving safety while maintaining high visual clarity.

High VisibilitySafety GlazingUV + Acoustic Support

Overview

Clear laminated glass delivers a dependable combination of transparency, impact retention, and day-to-day versatility. It is regularly specified where occupants, pedestrians, stock, or interior finishes benefit from an added level of safety and security.

In addition to retained-fragment performance, clear laminated glass can help reduce UV transmission and improve acoustic comfort compared with ordinary single glazing, depending on the final build-up selected for the project.

Key benefits

  • Maintains excellent optical clarity while adding safety performance.
  • Helps keep broken glass in place instead of allowing loose shards to fall free.
  • Supports UV reduction to help protect interiors, displays, and finishes.
  • Improves sound insulation compared with standard glass configurations.
  • Adds another barrier against penetration compared with standard float glass.

Typical Applications

  • Doors and sidelights
  • Windows and shopfronts
  • Balustrades and partitions
  • Interior glazing where clarity matters

Specification Note

Final thickness and interlayer selection should match the opening size, performance requirements, and any relevant safety or design criteria for the project.
