Toughened Product Detail

Toughened Glass Tables

Toughened glass table tops and furniture panels are used where elegant surface finishes need to be backed by dependable strength. They work well in residential and commercial environments where the glass will see direct daily contact.

Furniture GlassPolished EdgesDaily Durability

Overview

Furniture glass needs to feel refined without being fragile. Toughened table tops deliver that balance by combining a crisp, polished look with the strength needed for day-to-day domestic, hospitality, or office use.

Shapes, cut-outs, corner details, and edge finishes can be tailored to the furniture design, whether the glass is acting as the finished surface or as a protective top over another material.

Key benefits

  • Provides a clean, premium surface for tables and furniture pieces.
  • Offers improved impact resistance for everyday use.
  • Can be custom cut to shape with polished or featured edges.
  • Suitable for homes, boardrooms, hospitality, and display furniture.
  • Supports a wide range of base materials and furniture designs.

Typical Applications

  • Dining and coffee tables
  • Boardroom and office tables
  • Hospitality furniture
  • Protective glass tops for custom joinery

Specification Note

Glass thickness, edge detail, corner profile, and any cut-outs should be determined according to span, support conditions, and the intended furniture use.
