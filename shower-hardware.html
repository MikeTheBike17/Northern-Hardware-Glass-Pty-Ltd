Shower Hardware

Our shower hardware range features premium architectural components supplied by Schematech, designed specifically for laminated and toughened glass shower applications. The range supports reliable movement, stability, and long-term performance in wet environments.

Schematech SupplyFrameless SystemsPremium Finishes

Overview

By pairing our glass expertise with Schematech's precision-engineered hardware, we are able to support complete shower installations that balance design quality, reliable function, and wet-area durability. The range is intended for residential, hospitality, and custom shower projects that need dependable architectural hardware.

These systems are especially valuable for frameless and semi-frameless applications where the hardware plays a visible role in both performance and aesthetics.

Hardware options

  • Shower hinges including wall-to-glass, glass-to-glass, pivot, self-rising, and adjustable systems.
  • Shower clamps and brackets in bevel, flat, dome, and tee configurations.
  • Handles, knobs, and towel rails in square, round, pull, and flush formats.
  • Support arms and stabiliser bars with adjustable wall and glass mount systems.
  • Metal profiles, seals, magnetic systems, and complete sliding shower hardware solutions.

Finishes Available

  • Bright Chrome
  • Satin Chrome
  • Matte Black
  • Antique Brass
  • Natural Aluminium

Specification Note

Hardware selection should be matched to the glass type, panel configuration, opening direction, and design intent of the final shower installation.
