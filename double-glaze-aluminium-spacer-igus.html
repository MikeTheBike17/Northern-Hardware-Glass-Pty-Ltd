Aluminium Spacer IGUs

Aluminium spacer insulated glass units use a rigid, widely proven spacer profile to create the cavity between panes, offering dependable sealed-unit construction for applications where consistency, durability, and cost efficiency matter.

Proven SystemCost EfficientRigid Spacer

Overview

Aluminium spacer systems have been used for decades in insulated glass manufacture because they provide accurate cavity definition, strong dimensional stability, and compatibility with a broad range of glazing build-ups. The spacer also houses desiccants that help manage residual moisture within the sealed unit and preserve clarity.

These systems remain a dependable choice for projects where solid sealed-unit construction and cost effectiveness are prioritised, particularly in standard residential and commercial applications with moderate thermal demands.

Key benefits

  • Provides strong rigidity and consistent cavity spacing.
  • Offers a long-established, widely understood sealed-unit solution.
  • Supports effective moisture control through integrated desiccants.
  • Works well where budgets are important and thermal requirements are moderate.
  • Compatible with clear, tinted, laminated, toughened, and Low-E glass combinations.

Typical Applications

  • Standard residential windows and doors
  • Commercial glazing and shopfronts
  • Office partitions and internal glazing
  • Projects with cost-sensitive specifications

Specification Note

Where enhanced edge performance is not the primary driver, aluminium spacer systems remain a practical and economical solution for reliable insulated glazing construction.

System Detail

Available options and build flexibility

Aluminium spacer systems can be manufactured in a range of widths to suit different cavity requirements and can be paired with multiple pane types depending on the intended thermal, acoustic, or safety performance of the insulated unit.

  • Available in multiple spacer widths to match different cavity depths.
  • Can be supplied with air-filled or gas-filled cavities where specified.
  • Compatible with double- and triple-glazed builds.
  • Supports standard and oversized insulated glass panels.
  • Suitable for clear, tinted, laminated, toughened, and Low-E pane combinations.
