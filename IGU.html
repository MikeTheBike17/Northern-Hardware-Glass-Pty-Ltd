Insulated Glass Units

Warm Edge Technology

Warm Edge Technology is an advanced non-metal, flexible spacer system used in insulated glass units (IGUs / DGUs). Unlike traditional aluminium spacers, warm-edge spacers are designed to reduce heat loss around the perimeter of the unit (the "edge of glass"), improving thermal comfort, condensation resistance, and long-term performance.

Why the spacer matters:
In a sealed unit, the spacer forms the boundary between the panes and supports the air/gas cavity. The edge area is typically where the most heat transfer occurs, so improving the spacer system can significantly reduce energy loss and improve the overall performance of the window.

Key benefits of Warm Edge Technology include:

  • Reduces thermal bridging at the glass edge
    The non-metal design limits heat flow through the perimeter, helping keep the inner pane warmer in winter and improving comfort near windows.
  • Lower risk of condensation and cold-edge effects
    By increasing the internal glass edge temperature, warm-edge spacers reduce the likelihood of condensation forming around the edges.
  • Improved energy efficiency and comfort
    Better edge insulation supports more stable indoor temperatures and reduces reliance on heating/cooling.
  • Enhanced seal durability and longevity
    A flexible spacer helps accommodate expansion and contraction caused by temperature changes, supporting better long-term seal performance.
  • Improved acoustic performance (in many configurations)
    A high-quality spacer and correct DGU build can contribute to better sound reduction - especially when paired with laminated glass and varied pane thicknesses.



Where it's used

  • Residential windows and doors (improved comfort and condensation resistance)
  • Commercial glazing systems (better energy performance and long-term reliability)
  • Coastal or high-humidity environments (condensation management)
  • Energy-efficient builds and upgrades (low-energy homes, improved insulation performance)
  • Skylights and large glazed openings where comfort and edge performance matter

Available options & configurations

  • Different cavity sizes (spacer widths) based on performance and system requirements
  • Air-filled or gas-filled cavities (where specified)
  • Clear, tinted, Low-E, laminated or toughened pane combinations
  • Custom sizing for standard windows, large panels, and specialised glazing requirements

Aluminium Spacer Systems

Aluminium Spacer Systems are a traditional and widely used spacer solution in insulated glass units (IGUs / DGUs). Manufactured from formed aluminium profiles, these spacers create the cavity between glass panes and play a critical role in maintaining structural integrity, cavity spacing, and overall unit stability. Aluminium spacers have long been trusted for their strength, consistency, and proven performance in a wide range of glazing applications, particularly where durability and cost-effectiveness are key considerations.

Why aluminium spacers are still widely used:
The spacer defines the cavity depth and supports the sealed unit, housing desiccants that help control internal moisture. Aluminium spacer systems are rigid, dimensionally stable, and compatible with a broad range of glass types and glazing systems.

Key characteristics of Aluminium Spacer Systems include:

  • High structural rigidity and dimensional stability
    Aluminium spacers provide excellent strength and maintain consistent cavity widths, supporting accurate and repeatable DGU construction.
  • Proven performance and reliability
    With decades of industry use, aluminium spacers offer a well-understood and trusted solution for standard insulated glass applications.
  • Effective moisture control
    Integrated desiccants help absorb residual moisture within the sealed unit, reducing internal condensation and maintaining clarity.
  • Cost-effective spacer solution
    Aluminium spacer systems are an economical choice for projects where thermal performance requirements are moderate and budgets are a key factor.
  • Compatibility with a wide range of glazing systems
    Suitable for use with clear, tinted, laminated, toughened, and Low-E glass in various residential and commercial applications.



Where Aluminium Spacer Systems are commonly used

  • Standard residential windows and doors
  • Commercial glazing and shopfront installations
  • Office partitions and internal glazing systems
  • Projects where cost efficiency is prioritised over enhanced thermal performance
  • Applications with controlled indoor environments and lower condensation risk

Available options & configurations

  • Multiple spacer widths to suit different cavity sizes and performance requirements
  • Air-filled or gas-filled cavities (where specified)
  • Compatible with double or triple glazing builds
  • Custom sizing for standard and oversized glass panels
