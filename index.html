Since 1978 • Glass Industry Leader

Premium glass solutions for homes, businesses & more.

Northern Hardware & Glass is a proud Company Established in 1978. As one of the leaders in the Glass Industry in South Africa, we have expanded our business to include 12 branches specializing in Retail, Wholesale and Manufacturing. We pride ourselves in delivering glass products of outstanding quality and of the highest standard to our customers. From frameless showers to shopfronts and safety glass, we deliver clarity, safety and durability.

Your satisfaction is our promise to you!

Our vast range of glass products are manufactured with the highest professionalism and our ability to apply continuous innovation in specialized products with strict levels of the highest standard makes our company unique in the South African Glass Market.
