Balustrading - Flat & Curved

Toughened glass balustrading offers a clean, contemporary barrier solution for stairs, balconies, landings, and poolside edges. It is chosen where safety, strength, and uninterrupted sightlines need to work together in one architectural detail.

Structural SafetyFlat + Curved OptionsClear Views

Overview

Toughened balustrading is manufactured for applications where the glazing must stand up to regular use, wind loading, and occupant contact while preserving an open architectural feel. Flat panels suit streamlined contemporary layouts, while curved glass introduces a softer, premium finish where the design calls for it.

Depending on the project, the glass can be supplied for framed, semi-frameless, or frameless systems, with sizing and processing tailored to the installation method and required performance.

Key benefits

  • Creates strong barrier protection without adding visual heaviness.
  • Supports flat and curved designs for standard and bespoke projects.
  • Delivers safer breakage behaviour than ordinary annealed glass.
  • Works well in residential, hospitality, and commercial environments.
  • Can be paired with polished edges and hardware-ready processing.

Typical Applications

  • Balconies and terraces
  • Stair balustrades
  • Pool and leisure areas
  • Commercial walkway guarding

Specification Note

Final thickness, fixing system, and panel sizing should always be selected against the structural requirement, opening dimensions, and relevant safety standards for the project.
