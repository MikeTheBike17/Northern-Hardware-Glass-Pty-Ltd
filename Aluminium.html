About The Range

Aluminium and hardware selected to support safe, efficient, and polished glass installations.

Our aluminium and hardware products are chosen to support the practical demands of glazing installations across residential, commercial, and specialised work. From structural profiles and fixing accessories to premium hardware systems, the range is intended to deliver compatibility, reliability, and a cleaner finished result.

We also work with trusted suppliers such as Schematech for premium shower hardware, allowing us to pair our glass expertise with proven components for frameless and semi-frameless systems.

Typical Benefits

  • Supports safer, more stable installation of glass across multiple applications.
  • Corrosion-resistant and suitable for interior and exterior environments.
  • Available in multiple finishes for architectural consistency.
  • Compatible with modern shower, partition, balustrade, and shopfront systems.
  • Backed by product guidance from experienced sales teams and glazing specialists.
