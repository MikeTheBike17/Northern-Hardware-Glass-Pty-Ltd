Kitchen Splashbacks (Painted Glass)

Painted glass kitchen splashbacks offer a sleek alternative to traditional tiles while delivering a smooth, easy-to-clean surface. The glass is painted on the rear face and sealed to create a clean, modern finish suited to contemporary kitchens.

Custom ColoursSeamless FinishEasy Maintenance

Overview

Painted splashback glass is a popular choice where kitchens need a refined, low-maintenance wall finish instead of grout lines and segmented tile layouts. Because the surface is non-porous and continuous, it is straightforward to wipe down and helps keep the space feeling cleaner and more contemporary.

The glass can be custom cut around outlets, fixtures, and appliance zones, making it well suited to both residential kitchens and more design-conscious commercial environments.

Key benefits

  • Suitable for residential and commercial kitchen installations.
  • Provides a smooth, non-porous surface that is easy to clean and maintain.
  • Resistant to moisture, stains, and everyday kitchen splashes.
  • Can be custom cut to suit outlets, fixtures, and appliance layouts.
  • Available in a wide range of colours to match cabinetry and interior finishes.

Typical Applications

  • Residential kitchen splashbacks
  • Commercial kitchen feature walls
  • Sculleries and food prep zones
  • Colour-coordinated interior upgrades

Specification Note

Final cut-outs, fixing method, and colour selection should be confirmed against the finished kitchen layout so the splashback aligns cleanly with sockets, fittings, and cabinetry.

Colour Options

Painted splashbacks can be produced in a wide range of colours to coordinate with cabinets, counters, and surrounding finishes.
