Laminated Safety Glass - Rainbow

Rainbow laminated safety glass uses decorative or coloured interlayers between panes of glass to create striking visual effects while preserving the essential safety benefits of laminated construction. It is ideal for architectural glazing where appearance matters as much as performance.

Decorative InterlayerFeature GlazingArchitectural Colour

Overview

This laminated product allows designers to introduce colour, diffusion, and layered visual depth without giving up the protective qualities that make laminated glass a dependable safety option. The exact final appearance can shift with lighting conditions, making it especially effective in statement installations.

Rainbow laminated glass is often chosen for projects where glazing needs to communicate brand, atmosphere, or a distinct architectural identity while still functioning as a durable building component.

Key benefits

  • Creates vivid colour effects and decorative depth through laminated interlayers.
  • Retains laminated safety performance if breakage occurs.
  • Helps filter UV while protecting interior finishes.
  • Supports creative use in both functional and purely visual applications.
  • Available in a range of modern colour expressions.

Typical Applications

  • Feature walls and interior partitions
  • Balustrades and decorative facades
  • Reception areas, displays, and artistic installations
  • Projects combining safety glazing with a stronger design signature

Design Note

Colour, opacity, and light transmission should be considered early so the laminated build-up aligns with the intended mood, daylight conditions, and final viewing angles.
