Aluminium & Hardware

Our aluminium and glass hardware range is designed to deliver strength, precision, and long-term reliability across residential, commercial, and industrial applications. From framing systems and fittings to specialised hardware solutions, we supply components that ensure durability and a clean professional finish to meet demanding performance requirements while complementing modern architectural and functional glass systems.

Why Choose Our Aluminium & Glass Hardware?

  • Engineered to support glass safely and securely in fixed and moving applications.
  • Suitable for indoor and outdoor use, including coastal and high-humidity environments.
  • Designed to work seamlessly with laminated, toughened, and insulated glass systems.
  • Available in a range of finishes to suit architectural designs.

Aluminium Profiles & Accessories

Shower Hardware

Our shower hardware range features premium architectural components supplied by Schematech, a leading manufacturer of high-performance glass hardware systems. Designed specifically for laminated and toughened glass applications, Schematech hardware ensures strength, durability, and smooth operation in demanding wet environments.

By combining our glass expertise with Schematech’s precision-engineered hardware, we deliver complete frameless and semi-frameless shower solutions built for safety, stability, and long-term performance.

Through our partnership with Schematech, we are able to supply a comprehensive range of architectural glass hardware, including:

  • Shower Hinges – Wall-to-glass, glass-to-glass, pivot, self-rising, and adjustable systems
  • Shower Clamps & Brackets – Bevel, flat, dome and tee configurations
  • Handles, Knobs & Towel Rails – Pull handles, flush handles, square and round designs
  • Support Arms & Stabiliser Bars – Adjustable wall and glass mount systems
  • Metal Profiles – U-channels, compensation channels, flat bars, header rails
  • Seals & Magnetic Systems – Soft lip, hard lip, bubble seals and magnetic closures
  • Sliding Shower Systems – Complete sliding hardware solutions

Available in a range of finishes including Bright Chrome, Satin Chrome, Matte Black, Antique Brass, and Natural Aluminium, ensuring compatibility with modern residential, hospitality, and commercial projects.

More About Our Aluminium & Hardware

Our aluminium and glass hardware solutions are selected to support the safe, efficient, and professional installation of glass systems across a wide range of applications. From everyday glazing requirements to specialised project needs, we supply reliable components that meet industry standards and real-world performance demands.
