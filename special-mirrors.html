Specialised Product Detail

Mirrors (Sheets & Custom Cuts)

Our mirrors are supplied in full sheets or custom-cut to size, giving projects the flexibility to meet both functional and decorative requirements. Precision cutting and finishing help achieve clean edges and a high-quality reflective finish across a wide range of applications.

Custom CutsPolished + BevelledDecorative + Functional

Overview

Mirror glass can serve a wide range of purposes, from practical daily-use installations to bespoke interior features. We supply mirrors in full sheets or cut to the project specification, making them suitable for bathrooms, bedrooms, gyms, retail spaces, and decorative wall treatments.

Custom shapes, polished edges, and bevel details can all be coordinated to suit the intended installation and visual style of the space.

Key benefits

  • Suitable for bathrooms, bedrooms, gyms, retail spaces, and interior features.
  • Available as full sheets or custom-cut shapes and sizes.
  • Can be polished, bevelled, or edge-finished to specification.
  • Provides clear, high-quality reflection with a durable backing.
  • Ideal for both practical use and decorative wall applications.

Typical Applications

  • Bathrooms and bedrooms
  • Gyms and wellness spaces
  • Retail and showroom interiors
  • Decorative wall mirrors and features

Specification Note

Frames, backing boards, and mirror encasings are not included as standard, but these can be arranged on request depending on the installation requirement.
