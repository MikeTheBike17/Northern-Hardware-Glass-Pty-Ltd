Aluminium Product Detail

Aluminium Profiles & Accessories

We supply a comprehensive range of square aluminium profiles and accessories selected to support laminated and toughened glass installations across residential, commercial, and specialised applications. The range is chosen for precision, strength, and long-term durability.

System ComponentsCorrosion ResistantMulti-Application

Overview

Our aluminium range is intended to support glass systems across a wide spectrum of installations, including balustrades, shopfronts, shower enclosures, partitions, mirrors, and more specialised project work. These components are selected to work practically with our broader glass offering while maintaining strength, consistency, and ease of installation.

Because the range includes both primary profiles and supporting accessories, it helps keep finished glazing systems aligned in appearance and dependable in use.

Key benefits

  • Compatible with laminated, toughened, and double-glazed glass units.
  • Precision-engineered for strength, stability, and ease of installation.
  • Corrosion-resistant aluminium suitable for interior and exterior use.
  • Available in multiple finishes including natural, anodised, and powder-coated options.
  • Ideal for shopfronts, windows, doors, balustrades, partitions, shower enclosures, and display systems.

Profiles & Components Supplied

  • Square aluminium profiles
  • Square channels and U-profiles
  • Square frames and trims
  • Mounting and fixing accessories
  • Glazing support components

Specification Note

Our aluminium stock is selected to provide structural reliability, installation efficiency, and a consistent finish across the glass systems it supports.
