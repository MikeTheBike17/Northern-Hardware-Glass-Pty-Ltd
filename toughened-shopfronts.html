Toughened Product Detail

Shopfronts

Toughened glass shopfront systems help create bright, welcoming commercial frontages while offering the strength and durability needed for busy public environments. They support clean retail presentation without compromising on everyday safety performance.

Commercial GlazingHigh VisibilityImpact Ready

Overview

Shopfront glazing needs to perform under constant use while presenting merchandise and interiors clearly to passing foot traffic. Toughened glass is a strong choice here because it combines good optical clarity with higher impact resistance and safer breakage behaviour than ordinary glass.

Panels can be produced in sizes and finishes suited to frameless entrances, glazed screens, and branded retail frontages, with processing selected to suit patch fittings, door systems, and surrounding framing details.

Key benefits

  • Creates open, light-filled frontage designs with excellent visibility.
  • Offers improved strength for busy commercial environments.
  • Supports safer breakage characteristics if the glass is damaged.
  • Can be processed for door systems, cut-outs, and hardware fittings.
  • Works well for retail, hospitality, office, and showroom projects.

Typical Applications

  • Retail shopfronts
  • Commercial entrances
  • Showrooms and dealerships
  • Hospitality glazing screens

Specification Note

Door integration, opening sizes, hardware positions, and site exposure should all be considered when selecting the final toughened shopfront specification.
