Laminated Reflective Glass

Laminated reflective glass combines laminated safety glass with a reflective coating that helps control solar heat and glare while creating a more private exterior appearance during the day. It is especially suited to projects where solar performance and visual impact are both important.

Solar ControlDaytime PrivacyCommercial Facades

Overview

Reflective laminated glass is a practical specification for commercial and high-exposure architectural projects. It helps manage incoming sunlight and contributes to daytime privacy, while the laminated structure continues to support safety and security goals.

Because it combines appearance and function so effectively, it is often chosen for office buildings, shopfronts, facades, and larger glazed elevations.

Key benefits

  • Reduces solar heat gain and glare by reflecting part of the incoming sunlight.
  • Supports daytime privacy from the outside.
  • Retains laminated safety and security behavior.
  • Helps limit UV-related fading inside the building.
  • Available in several reflective finishes to suit the architectural intent.

Typical Applications

  • Commercial facades and office buildings
  • Shopfronts and sun-exposed glazing
  • Projects needing privacy and a stronger facade identity
  • Large glazed elevations with solar control requirements

Available Finishes

  • Silver reflective
  • Bronze reflective
  • Grey reflective
  • Black reflective
  • Low-E reflective
