Sound Reducing Acoustic Glass

Acoustic laminated glass uses a specialised sound-dampening interlayer to reduce the transfer of noise through glazing. It is made for spaces where quietness, comfort, and privacy matter just as much as safety.

Noise ReductionComfortPrivacy

Overview

Acoustic laminated glass is designed to dampen sound vibration passing through the glazing system. Depending on the thickness and final configuration selected, it can reduce transmitted noise significantly and help create calmer interior environments.

This makes it a strong option for projects near roads, airports, commercial activity, or any setting where sound control improves the quality and usability of the interior.

Key benefits

  • Helps reduce noise transmission by roughly 30-45 dB depending on specification.
  • Improves privacy and occupant comfort in busy environments.
  • Maintains laminated safety performance.
  • Can be combined with tinted or Low-E options for broader performance.
  • Supports higher-quality interior conditions without closing off daylight.

Typical Applications

  • Homes near busy roads or airports
  • Offices, hotels, and healthcare environments
  • Studios and privacy-sensitive spaces
  • Urban projects needing quieter interiors

Specification Note

Acoustic performance depends on the full glazing system, including glass make-up, framing, seals, and installation quality.
